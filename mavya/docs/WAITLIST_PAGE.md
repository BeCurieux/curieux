# Ovyko — waitlist page copy

The words for a "join the waitlist" page for swim school owners, for the
pre-launch month in `docs/LAUNCH_90_DAYS.md`. It can be its own page
(ovyko.com.au/founding) or replace the "Book a demo" section of the
current home page. Plain language, no claims we can't back up.

Placeholders in [brackets]; numbers only once they're real and we have
permission to use them.

## The page

**Headline**
> Your swim school, on autopilot.

**Sub-headline**
> Ovyko fills places that would sit empty, collects your fees and chases
> the late ones, so your front desk stops doing it by hand.

**Button**
> Join the founding schools waitlist

**Three reasons (short, one line each)**
- **Absences become filled places.** A parent taps "can't make it", books
  a make-up that fits your rules, and their spot is offered to another
  family. Nobody at the desk touches it.
- **Fees that collect themselves.** Card, direct debit or instalments,
  reminders before and after the due date, and failed payments chased
  for you.
- **Re-enrolment in one tap.** Families confirm next term from their
  phone, and you see who's staying before the term starts.

**See what it did for you**
> At the end of every month, Ovyko shows you what it handled: places
> filled, fees collected, overdue fees paid, families staying, and the
> hours your desk got back.

(Once the pilot has real figures, and the owner's permission: "In
[month], [Pilot school] filled N places that would have sat empty.")

**Moving in is the scary part, so we do it**
> We move your classes and families in from your current system, show
> you the counts match, and run alongside it until you're sure. No paying
> twice while your old contract runs out.

**Built for Australian schools**
> Your data stays in Australia. Health notes reach only the instructors
> who need them. Owners sign in with two-step codes. Families can
> download or delete their data when they ask.

(Keep this only once the "confirm everything runs in Australia" item in
`docs/OWNER_TODO.md` is done.)

**Founding schools**
> We're opening to [10] swim schools in [area] before [next term].
> Founding schools get the move-in free, the first term free, and
> A$399 a month per location held for two years.
>
> We can only move a few schools in properly at once, so it's first come.

(The owner of Ovyko decides the offer; this is the suggestion from
`docs/LAUNCH_90_DAYS.md`.)

**From the founder** (short and real, in the owner of Ovyko's own words;
a starting point)
> I started Ovyko after hearing the same thing from swim school owners:
> the lessons are the easy part. It's the absences, the make-up phone
> calls, the fees and the re-enrolment forms that eat the week. Ovyko is
> built to make those jobs happen on their own. I'd love to hear how they
> work at your school.
> [Your name], founder

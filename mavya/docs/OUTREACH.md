# Ovyko — outreach messages

Messages for reaching swim school owners, in the spirit of the Sash Beds
playbook: personal, short, specific to the person, never a copy-paste
blast. Use with `docs/LAUNCH_90_DAYS.md`.

Fill in everything in [brackets]. If you can't find something specific to
say about a school, don't send the message yet.

## Staying within the rules

- **Australian spam law (Spam Act 2003):** a commercial email or message
  needs the person's consent. Consent can be inferred when a business
  publishes its email address conspicuously and the message is relevant
  to their role (a swim school's public enquiries address, about running
  a swim school). Every message must say who it's from and how to contact
  you, and offer a simple way to say "no more" that you honour.
- One person, one message, one follow-up. No bought lists, no automated
  sequences to strangers.
- Never claim results we haven't measured, never invent quotes, and only
  name the pilot school with its owner's written permission.

## 1. First message (email or a message to the school's page)

Ovyko is made on the Central Coast; say "here on the Coast" to Central
Coast schools and "just up the road on the Central Coast" elsewhere. No
call is asked for: the owner can look in their own time with "Try it
yourself" on the website (`docs/TRY_IT_YOURSELF.md`), and reply by email.

**Subject:** A little app for [School name], made on the Central Coast

> Hi [Owner's first name],
>
> I hope the new term's off to a good start!
>
> I'm [Your name], and I've been building Ovyko here on the Central Coast.
> It's a simple app for swim schools that takes care of the fiddly bits:
> absences, make-ups, waiting lists and chasing fees, so you and your team
> get your evenings back.
>
> I came across [something specific: your Facebook post asking families to
> swap make-up times / the waitlist on your Junior Squad page / that
> you've opened a second pool in X] and thought you might like a look.
>
> There's a 90-second video, or you can click around a pretend swim
> school of your own, at www.ovyko.com.au ("Try it yourself").
>
> If anything catches your eye, just hit reply. And I'd love to know how
> you handle make-ups at [School name] today; even a one-line answer helps
> me build the right thing.
>
> Thanks so much,
> [Your name]
> Ovyko · hello@ovyko.com.au
>
> P.S. If it's not for you, no worries at all. Just reply "no thanks" and
> I won't email again.

## 6. Asking the pilot owner for an introduction

> Hi [Pilot owner], you've been a huge help with the pilot. Is there an
> owner you respect who you think would want to see what Ovyko's done at
> [School]? A short intro email is plenty; I'll take it from there, and
> I won't hassle them.

## The demo, in 20 minutes

1. **Their week first (5 min).** "Walk me through what happened the last
   time a family couldn't make a lesson."
2. **The same moment in Ovyko (5 min).** Parent taps "can't make it", the
   credit, the make-up, the spot offered and filled.
3. **Money (5 min).** Fees, a reminder, a failed direct debit chased,
   instalments; then "This month with Ovyko".
4. **Moving in (3 min).** Import, the counts that prove it matches,
   running alongside their current system.
5. **Next step (2 min).** "Would you want to be one of the founding
   schools? When's your next term break?"

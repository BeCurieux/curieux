# OVYKO v0.1 — Product Definition

## Working proposition

**Everything they do, together.**

Ovyko connects families with businesses that run recurring children's activities.

### Parent value

One simple place for:

- weekly schedule
- absences
- make-ups
- progress
- messages
- payments later
- multiple children

### Provider value

One calm operating layer for:

- classes
- families
- attendance
- make-ups
- progress
- capacity
- waitlists
- communications
- payments later

## First market

Recurring weekly classes with levels and capacity constraints.

Initial design partners should come from:

- swimming
- gymnastics
- dance

Do not optimise for soccer leagues, camps, tutoring, music schools or franchises yet.

## First proof loop

1. Business creates recurring class.
2. Family/child is enrolled.
3. Parent reports absence.
4. Ovyko checks make-up policy.
5. Valid make-up credit is issued.
6. Original occurrence gains temporary capacity.
7. Parent sees valid make-up classes.
8. Parent books one.
9. Another eligible family may claim the temporary vacancy.
10. Instructor takes attendance and updates skill progress.
11. Parent sees progress update.

## Hero B2B capability

### Fill Empty Spots

Ovyko should identify temporary vacancies and match them to eligible children with make-up credits.

The value proposition is not "better admin".

It is:

> **Fill classes. Retain families. Do less admin.**

## v0.1 non-goals

Do not build:

- marketplace
- activity discovery
- social feed
- loyalty
- parent subscription
- native mobile apps
- franchise hierarchy
- payroll
- staff HR
- Xero
- league fixtures
- sophisticated AI recommendations
- universal family optimisation
- cross-provider payments

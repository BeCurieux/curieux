**[Business name]** ("we") makes Tildie, a Shopify app that reviews the wording
of a store's product pages against advertising rules. This policy explains what
the app collects when a merchant installs it, and what it does with it.

## What we collect

- **Your store's domain**, which Shopify gives us when you install the app.
- **Your product copy**: each product's title, description, search-engine
  description, handle, status and web address, read through Shopify with the
  `read_products` permission. We read nothing else from your store — no orders,
  no customers, no payment details.
- **The results of our review**: the scores, notes and marks we produce from
  that copy, and the markets you asked us to review against.
- **An access token** Shopify issues so the app can read your products. It is
  encrypted before it is stored, with a key held separately from the database.
- **Your plan**, as Shopify reports it.
- **A short record of the notifications Shopify sends us** (for example, that
  a product changed), kept for seven days so the same one is not processed
  twice.

We do not collect personal information about your customers. When a shopper
views a product page carrying the mark, the image is requested through your own
store's address; we do not set cookies and do not identify the shopper.

## What we do with it

We use it only to provide the app: to read and review your product copy, show
you the results, and show the mark on pages that have earned it. We do not sell
it, and we do not use your copy to train models.

## Where it is kept

With Supabase (database, United States) and Vercel (hosting, United States).

## How long

While the app is installed. When you uninstall, the mark stops appearing
immediately; Shopify then asks us to delete your store's data, and we delete
everything we hold for it.

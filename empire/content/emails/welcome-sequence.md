## Email 5 · Day 10 · Three ways a test can end

**Subject:** Go, adjust, stop, or "not yet"
**Preview:** Why "not enough evidence" is a real answer.

Hi [first name],

When you test an idea, people expect two endings: it worked, or it didn't. There's a third that matters more at the start: not enough evidence yet.

Fewer than five real conversations with possible customers isn't a no. It's too few to know. So our plans end with one of four honest verdicts: go, adjust, stop, or keep testing because there isn't enough evidence yet.

Wherever you are with your idea, I'd like to know one thing: what's the part you're least sure about? Hit reply and tell me. I read every reply, and it shapes what we make next.

That's the end of this welcome series. From here, you'll only hear from us when there's something new worth your time.

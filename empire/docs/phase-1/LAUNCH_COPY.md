### Who it's for

- You want to earn your own money around a real life: school hours, a day job, a toddler, limited evenings.
- You'd rather know the costs and the rules before you start than find out later.
- You're happy to test an idea for 14 days instead of guessing.

### Who it isn't for

- Anyone looking for a promise of income. Nobody honest can make one, and we don't.
- Anyone after a scheme where you earn by signing other people up. Pay-to-join schemes are on our red-flag list.

### What's inside

- **Your Income Fit Report:** the 20-question quiz, your top three of 23 researched ideas, a calculator for each idea, the rules for five countries, a dated 14-day plan, and how to decide at the end.
- **The workbook**, to work through it on paper or in a spreadsheet.
- **23 Opportunity Cards:** one researched card per idea, each with the date it was checked.
- **The 14-Day Test Plan:** one objective, one task and one output a day, 30 to 60 minutes.
- **The Go / No-Go Scorecard:** for Day 14.
- **The AI Shortcut System:** prompts that speed up the research and writing.
- **Rules Guides:** Australia, the US, the UK, Canada and New Zealand: registration, hobby or business, records, tax, GST/VAT thresholds, insurance and licences, and where to get free help, each item with the date it was checked.

**Plus, in Empire Studio:** sign in with the email you bought with, and the same report works as an interactive version that saves your answers and your 14-day plan.

### How it works

1. **Answer 20 questions** about your week, money, skills and limits. About 15 minutes (EST).
2. **Read your top three:** why each fits, what it costs, the rules where you live.
3. **Test one for 14 days,** then decide with four honest outcomes: go, adjust, stop, or not enough evidence yet.

### What it won't do

It won't tell you whether your customers will pay; only a real test can, which is what the 14-day plan is for. It won't give you an income figure to expect. Every number in it is labelled FACT (checked, with a date), EST (an estimate) or UNKNOWN.

### Questions

**Will this make me money?**
It can't promise that, and neither can anyone else. It helps you choose an idea that fits and test it cheaply, so you find out quickly and spend little.

**I'm not in Australia. Does it work for me?**
The rules guides cover five countries. The quiz asks where you live and sets the currency. Somewhere else? The quiz works in US$, but the rules are yours to check.

**What if I already have an idea?**
Good: score it against your week, and use the 14-day plan to test it.

**How long does it take?**
The quiz is about 15 minutes (EST). The test is 14 days of 30 to 60 minutes (EST).

**Can I get a refund?**

## 3. Product quality notes

- **Originality:** the course and tools are original; they reuse only HLE's own methods (Price It Right fee maths, the "not enough evidence" rule, one-variable testing), cited in `docs/HLE_EXISTING_ASSETS_AUDIT.md`.
- **Exercises and worked examples:** Module 1 has a checklist, a five-question assessment with answers and four hypothetical AUD examples, two of them losses. The kits have worked examples per the register.
- **Honesty:** every worked example is labelled hypothetical; no result is presented as a customer outcome.
- **Weakest point:** everything new is either software without a buy button or a course with one module. The fastest real value is the **calculator page**, because the hard part (the maths) is done and tested.

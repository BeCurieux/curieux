# Meta ad creative briefs (drafts, not launched)

**Nothing here is live, and no campaign may be created or launched without the founder's written budget approval.** Meta tracking isn't installed (FUNNEL_MEASUREMENT.md §4). Until it is, results are read from Ads Manager plus our own UTM-tagged landing events, not from a pixel.

**Before any of these run:**

1. Check Meta's current advertising standards for income-related and "business opportunity" content, and for claims about personal attributes. **UNVERIFIED here.** Ads that imply someone's finances or promise outcomes are commonly restricted.
2. Studio is live on `studio.herlittleempire.com`, with the landing page working.
3. The founder sets a total test budget and a stop date.
4. Every line passes the claims guard (no income figures, promised outcomes or urgency) and the house rules.
5. Audio is either original voice-over or music licensed for ads. Instagram's in-app music library for regular posts doesn't cover ads.

**Faceless production for all three:**

- hands, desk, notebook, laptop showing our own tools (no personal data), kettle;
- text on screen in brand type (Bodoni-style headings on cream `#f7f2e9`, aubergine `#43252c`);
- captions burned in for sound-off viewing;
- no people's faces, no children, no stock footage of people, no AI-generated people.

## Brief 1: Anti-MLM profile discovery (awareness)

| Field       | Detail                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective   | Profile visits and follows from people who share the values: honest, numbers-first, no recruiting                                                                                   |
| Audience    | Broad, in the countries the Rules Guides cover (AU first), adults. Avoid targeting that implies personal hardship. Interests: UNVERIFIED (test broad first)                         |
| Placement   | Instagram Reels and Feed                                                                                                                                                            |
| Destination | The Instagram profile (`@herlittleempire.co`)                                                                                                                                       |
| Measure     | Cost per profile visit; profile visit → follow (Ads Manager + Insights); non-follower reach of the next unpaid posts                                                                |
| Kill rule   | Stop when the founder's test budget is spent, or after 7 days, whichever comes first. Judge only if there are enough visits to be meaningful; under that, "not enough evidence yet" |

**Concept: "The DM you didn't ask for."** A dry read of a generic "opportunity" message, decoded line by line.

**Script (about 20 seconds, EST):**

1. (0–2s) On screen: _"Hey hun 💕 have you ever thought about your own business?"_ Hand slides the phone face-down. VO: "We need to talk about this message."
2. (2–8s) On screen, one line at a time: _"'Be your own boss'"_ → "…with a boss above you." _"'Small starter kit'"_ → "…that you pay for." _"'Only takes an hour a day'"_ → "…plus finding the people under you."
3. (8–15s) VO: "If the money comes from signing people up, not selling something people want, it's a pay-to-join scheme. It's on our red-flag list." On screen: the red-flag list from the Fit Check (real page).
4. (15–20s) On screen: "Her Little Empire. Make your own money. Know what you're getting into." VO: "We show the numbers. Nobody has to recruit anyone."

**Primary text:** "The 'opportunity' DM, translated. We build honest tools for your own small business: real costs, real rules, and a 14-day test before you spend big. Nobody to recruit."
**Headline:** "Know what you're getting into"
**CTA button:** Visit Instagram profile (or Learn more → profile)
**Don't:** depict or name a real company or person; use the words the claims guard flags; show income screenshots.

## Brief 2: Free Fit Check lead generation

| Field           | Detail                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective       | Fit Check downloads and, once the opt-in is switched on, explicit email consents                                                                          |
| Audience        | Broad (AU first), adults; retarget profile engagers only if advertising consent and tracking are in place (BLOCKED today)                                 |
| Placement       | Instagram Reels, Stories                                                                                                                                  |
| Destination     | `https://studio.herlittleempire.com/fit-check?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=b2-fitcheck&utm_content=<creative>`                            |
| Measure         | Cost per landing view (Ads Manager clicks vs our `fit_check_viewed`); `offer_clicked` (to Gumroad); `email_consent_given`; Fit Check downloads in Gumroad |
| Value of a lead | **Unknown** until the nurture flow has sold enough to measure; judge on the founder's test budget, not on profit                                          |
| Kill rule       | Budget spent or 7 days. Pause any creative whose landing views per click is much lower than the others (a sign of a mismatch or in-app browser trouble)   |

**Concept: "Ten minutes. Five questions."** The Fit Check filled in by hand.

**Script (about 25 seconds, EST):**

1. (0–2s) On screen: "Most side-income ideas don't fail because they're bad." Pen hovering over a printed page.
2. (2–6s) "They fail because they don't fit the week you actually have."
3. (6–18s) Hands score three ideas on the real printable: hours a week, longest block, evenings, start-up money, talking to customers. On screen: "0, 1 or 2 each. Out of 10."
4. (18–22s) On screen: "8–10: test it first. 5–7: possible. 0–4: park it."
5. (22–25s) On screen: "Free. Link below." VO: "Ten minutes, before you spend a cent."

**Primary text:** "Score up to three side-income ideas against the week you actually have, in ten minutes. Six red flags included. Free printable."
**Headline:** "The free 5-Question Fit Check"
**CTA button:** Download (or Learn more)
**Don't:** promise income, imply the reader's situation ("struggling mum?"), or use countdowns.

## Brief 3: Ads Calculator education

| Field       | Detail                                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective   | Calculator visits from small sellers about to spend on ads; profile follows as a secondary result                                     |
| Audience    | Broad (AU first), adults interested in small online selling (interests UNVERIFIED; start broad)                                       |
| Placement   | Instagram Reels, Feed                                                                                                                 |
| Destination | `https://studio.herlittleempire.com/ads/tools/calculator?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=b3-calc&utm_content=<creative>` |
| Measure     | `calculator_viewed`; `offer_clicked` from the calculator; follows                                                                     |
| Kill rule   | Budget spent or 7 days                                                                                                                |

**Concept: "ROAS above 1, still losing."** Show the calculator's own hypothetical example.

**Script (about 25 seconds, EST):**

1. (0–2s) On screen: "My ads made more than they cost. So why am I losing money?"
2. (2–10s) Screen recording of the calculator with the example: A$49 download, Gumroad fee 10% + A$0.80, 3% refunds → "You keep A$41.83 per sale" (hypothetical).
3. (10–18s) Add A$120 of ads, 2 sales → "ROAS 0.82 · after ads −A$36.34". On screen: "Break-even ROAS here is 1.17, not 1."
4. (18–22s) On screen: "And 2 sales isn't enough evidence either way."
5. (22–25s) On screen: "Free calculator. Your numbers stay in your browser."

**Primary text:** "Before you spend on ads: the most you can pay for one sale and still make money, after fees, GST and refunds. Free, and nothing you type leaves your browser. (Example figures are hypothetical.)"
**Headline:** "Know your break-even before you boost"
**CTA button:** Learn more

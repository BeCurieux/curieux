**Default production notes (apply to every Reel unless the piece says otherwise):**

- Faceless: hands, desk, screens with non-identifying content, notebook, mug, kettle. No children, no school, no reflections, no location clues. Run the faceless checklist in the content system.

- Captions: burned in, Australian spelling, checked by hand. Auto-captions on as well.
- Lengths are estimates (EST). Cut to the script, not to the clock.
- Hashtags: 0–3 at most, effect UNKNOWN. Captions below are written without them; add up to three plain ones if testing.

### Day 5 · Carousel · AUD · Red flags worth stopping for

**Slides:**

1. Red flags worth stopping for. (Before you spend a cent.)
2. **It needs a licence you don't have.** Some work needs registration or a licence. Check the rules where you live before you start, not after a complaint.

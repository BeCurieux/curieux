# Five sales assets (Reels)

**Video files:** `docs/sales/assets/01-fit-check.mp4` … `05-what-you-get.mp4`, each with a `-cover.jpg`. Each one:

- is 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, silent;
- is recorded frame by frame from the **real product screens** (Empire Studio, local build), by `tests/e2e/zz-social-assets.spec.ts`;
- has the captions below burned in, in brand type, so it works with the sound off.

**Approved copy:** every caption line comes from the founder-reviewed launch copy (`docs/phase-1/LAUNCH_COPY.md`), the calendar (`docs/marketing/30_DAY_CONTENT_CALENDAR.md`) or the calculator's own worked example.

The test `tests/unit/sales.test.ts` runs the claims guard over this file, and checks that the exact refund sentence accompanies every US$19 mention.

**What these are not:**

- **No original filmed footage** (hands, desk, notebook). That needs the founder's camera. The filmed scripts are in the 30-day calendar.
- **No audio.** Add original voice-over, or Instagram's in-app music for regular posts only; that music doesn't cover ads.
- **No fake results:** the report shown belongs to a test account named "Sam". The calculator figures are hypothetical and labelled.

**Before publishing:**

1. Studio is live at `studio.herlittleempire.com` (the end cards say "Link in bio").
2. The bio link points at the tagged Fit Check page (SEVEN_DAY_CAMPAIGN.md).
3. Watch each file once on a phone.
4. The founder approves.
